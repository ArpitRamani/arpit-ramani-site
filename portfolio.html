ALS Spinal Cord Proteomics

Differential expression over a sparse 34-patient cohort in R.

RlimmaTAMPORFragPipe

details
Role
Computational proteomics research intern, Dr. Blaine Roberts Lab, Emory.
Problem
Nitro-tyrosine and nitro-tryptophan differential expression across the McEachin C9orf72 ALS spinal cord TMTpro set, 34 patients over two plexes.
Method
TAMPOR batch and loading normalization, a 50% missingness filter, random-forest imputation, then limma moderated t-tests under BH-FDR across three contrasts: C9 ALS against control, antisense-treated against control, and treated against untreated.
Result
TAMPOR keeps the ~124 sites detected in both plexes; a log2 median-centred fallback carries ~419 sparser sites with batch as a covariate. Both modes ship, because a sparse modification is often sampled in one plex only.

Nitration MD

Distributed molecular dynamics on AWS ParallelCluster GPU arrays.

OpenMMCUDAAMBERSlurm

details
Role
Computational proteomics research intern, Dr. Blaine Roberts Lab, Emory.
Question
Whether nitration is site-selective rather than random oxidative damage. Proton-coupled electron transfer predicts that nitrated aromatics sit beside a carboxylate proton acceptor and a thiol redox partner.
Method
AMBER ff19SB/OPC systems built through AmberTools and run on OpenMM and CUDA at a 4 fs timestep under hydrogen-mass repartitioning, with replicas fanned across Slurm GPU arrays on AWS ParallelCluster.
Result
Per-aromatic donor-to-carboxylate distance, hydrogen-bond occupancy, ring-to-thiol distance and solvent exposure, compared between nitrated and non-nitrated residues inside the same trajectory.
